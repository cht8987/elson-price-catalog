You: You're a small business owner or team of 1–20 people. You do repetitive tasks daily — answering the same questions, copying data between apps, writing similar content, processing orders.

💡 Which should you choose?

One-time packages solve "get it done once." Monthly retainers solve "keep doing it well."

A Full Rebrand at RM 30,000 one-time gives you the assets but no ongoing content, ads, or SEO. The Growth retainer at RM 10,800/month includes continuous content production, ad management, SEO, and data-driven iteration — essentially an entire marketing department for less than one senior hire.

Most clients start with a project package and transition to a retainer. That's the sweet spot.

Terms & Conditions

Standard terms that keep things fair and transparent for both sides.

Payment Terms

50% upfront, 50% on delivery for project work. Monthly retainers billed in advance on the 1st of each month. Bank transfer or PayNow accepted.

Revision Policy

Each service includes the revision rounds stated above. Additional rounds billed at RM 500/round. Minor tweaks (colours, copy adjustments) within scope are included.

Cancellation

Project work: cancellation within 48 hours of kickoff results in a 25% admin fee. Monthly retainers: 30-day notice required. Unused ad budgets are fully refundable.

Ad Spend

Advertising budgets are paid directly to platforms (Meta, Google, etc.) and are not included in any service fees. I recommend and manage the budget but you control the spend.

Long-term Discount

12-month retainer commitment: 5% discount on monthly fees. 6-month: standard package rates. Longer commitment = better rate.

Travel & Accommodation

For on-site shoots or meetings outside KL: travel costs (transport + accommodation) billed at actuals. First visit is complimentary.
